# ADR-0017: Close the PrismPM Verification Gap with Governed Prism Stdlib Model Content

> **Local Identifier:** `ADR-0017` — recursive-cognition local register, succeeding local ADR-0016.  
> **Namespace:** `recursive-cognition-local`. No mount-wide or PRISM-global ADR number is asserted.  
> **Target Subsystem:** `/run/media/multiplic/AGI/prismagi/mount/SUBSYSTEMS/recursive-cognition`  
> **Supersession scope:** This ADR supersedes **only** ADR-0016 D7 and the matching clause of ADR-0015 D4 that said full `prismpm verify` was blocked. ADR-0016's Lean pin, LexLean pin, schemas, and historical attestation remain in force.  
> **Governing Plan:** [`docs/PLAN-ADR-0015.md`](PLAN-ADR-0015.md)  
> **Frozen Baseline:** `Multiplicity-Recursive Cognition Engine.txt` (v3.3 read-only external reference; not a production artifact and not linked because it is not present under `RC_ROOT`)  
> **Design Class:** `Base` governance decision with inherited `Safety-First` evidence obligations.

---

## 1. Context and Problem Statement

ADR-0016 resolved the Lean security-pin conflict and established LexLean `0.4.3` with Lean `leanprover/lean4:v4.32.2` as the canonical attestation stack. It did **not** close full PrismPM verification: its D7 recorded `PP5003: LexLean snapshot contains no Prism declarations`, and the governed root receipt `evidence/prismpm-050-verify-043-rust1971.stderr` reproduced that failure under the pinned Rust toolchain.

The blocker was model content, not governance format or the toolchain. `prismpm check` validates a governed project without requiring executable Prism declarations; `prismpm verify` additionally requires the LexLean snapshot to contain Prism declarations that can be lowered into Lean, checked by `leanchecker`, converted to LCNF, and recompiled as Rust. The four Phase 1 spike modules deliberately contain no such declarations.

At the same time the user requested installation of the latest `prism-code` launcher. `prism-code@0.11.1` was installed under the user-global npm prefix and `npx prism-code@latest init` was replayed in a scratch directory. A locally built PrismPM `0.11.1` was also evaluated, but it failed with `PP5008: required executable lake has an unpinned digest`. The canonical stack therefore remains `prismpm 0.5.0 check` plus real `prismpm verify`; the newer launcher is provenance, not a replacement verifier.

This ADR records the bounded model-content adoption that closes `PP5003` while preserving the canonical stack.

---

## 2. Decision Drivers

1. **Close the real blocker** — supply genuine Prism declarations to `prismpm verify`, not a bypass flag or a relaxed check.
2. **Preserve the canonical stack** — keep LexLean `0.4.3`, Lean `v4.32.2`, and PrismPM `0.5.0`; do not silently replace them with newer, unproven identities.
3. **Localization** — all adopted sources, licenses, vendor pins, receipts, ADRs, and evidence records resolve below `RC_ROOT`.
4. **Reproducibility** — the LexLean and Prism attestations must be identical on governed replay and after a clean rebuild.
5. **Supply-chain honesty** — upstream tag, commit, raw digest, canonicalized digest, and embedded exporter archive digest are recorded separately from production attestations.
6. **No unsupported claim** — passing `prismpm verify` closes the model-content gate only; it does not establish release, Kani, nine-suite, or TRR-2 readiness.
7. **Explicit supersession** — change ADR-0016 D7 and ADR-0015 D4 only through a linked ADR with retained historical receipts.

---

## 3. Decision Outcome

### 3.1 Y-statement

> In the context of **governed recursive-cognition verification after ADR-0016 closed the Lean pin conflict**,  
> facing **a reproducible `PP5003` model-content blocker in `prismpm verify`, a newer `prism-code` launcher, and a locally built PrismPM `0.11.1` that required an unpinned executable `lake`**,  
> we decided for **adopting pinned upstream Prism stdlib declarations under `module_prefix = "PrismPM"`, canonicalizing them with LexLean `0.4.3`, pinning the exact embedded exporter archive, and retaining PrismPM `0.5.0` as the canonical verifier**,  
> and against **claiming verification from `prismpm check` alone, switching to PrismPM `0.11.1`, or bypassing SDK pinning**,  
> to achieve **a real, reproducible `prismpm verify` pass under the governed stack**,  
> accepting **a larger verified model tree and a bounded scope that still excludes release and TRR-2 claims**.

### 3.2 Binding decisions

- **D1 — Canonical stack unchanged.** `lexlean 0.4.3`, `leanprover/lean4:v4.32.2`, `prismpm 0.5.0 check`, and real `prismpm verify` remain canonical. The binary digest in `tools.lock` is unchanged.
- **D2 — Prism namespace adopted.** `lexlean.toml:module_prefix = "PrismPM"` makes the adopted declarations visible as Prism declarations rather than local-only facets.
- **D3 — Upstream stdlib adopted and canonicalized.** The 115 `.lex.tex` files from upstream tag `prism-code-v0.11.1` commit `3b5ac767672959f48b3d5943f3e652b769439179` are kept under `stdlib/src/` and rewritten by `lexlean fmt --all`; the path-independent relative tree digests are `5b812ae7…` (raw) and `9a5d49f3…` (governed), and the path-dependent acquisition digest `b68e0964…` is retained as a decision input.
- **D4 — Exporter archive pinned.** `vendor/lean4-prod/lean.tar` is an exact regular file with digest `b1e282a8b41d8ce6172db40d73ded55e7d9db39b9145c3f5bbc160ce6c74100d`, matching the bytes embedded in the canonical `prismpm` binary.
- **D5 — Execution environment pinned.** `prismpm check/verify` runs with `RUSTUP_TOOLCHAIN=1.97.1`, `LC_ALL=C`, the canonical LexLean binary first on `PATH`, and `LL` set to that same binary. Default `rustfmt 1.11.0-stable` is rejected because it triggers `PP5008`.
- **D6 — LexLean attestation replaced.** The current CLI LexLean attestation is `7d2e6b4a9ceb7432a226d852f405a88ac07735a31897bee65f51d67ac721c8c5`, with source `5749419747de6b8392e55942f28140ba5dee45a853d80bccdac6b5f89aeb4cd7`, semantic `b4b1dbc5ebd11430e6c4619bc06781a3907fe3569962263daedb3cd816cc1563`, build `348f4b9be0da87b1e0792eb0fb921abd5f2400c9854ac138ae5b2eab3a247d25`, and `64` verified modules. PrismPM's verified root embeds the internally reproduced LexLean attestation `80d9539dce5e25317cdfd347b518468b16a42bc579a2678128765ec66279e21f` with the same source, semantic, and build IDs. The ADR-0016 digest remains historical.
- **D7 — PrismPM verify passes.** The current Prism attestation is `7963a1eb1ee4462fd5c87a7c1b2bc430dbbb2f8d0774b5dc606e43a0b2a66c71` (`prismpm/verify-result/1`) with build `f64f58390af6136a9ff6b1b835422459e580ebd6728e84f0654b752b7e8dd43a`, produced by real `prismpm verify` against the governed root.
- **D8 — Newer code is provenance only.** `prism-code@0.11.1` is installed globally and its `init` replay is retained, but it is not the canonical governance verifier. Built PrismPM `0.11.1` is rejected while it requires an unpinned `lake`.
- **D9 — Scope remains bounded.** This ADR closes `RISK-PRISM-MODEL-GAP` for the governed verification path only. Five v3.3 obligations, Kani harnesses, nine-suite runner, dual reports, pre-commit/CI gates, release publication, and TRR-2 sign-offs remain unclaimed.

## 5. Alternatives Considered

| Option | Result | Disposition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Leave `PP5003` in place and treat `prismpm check` as verification | Check passes but verify remains a real failure. | **Rejected.** Violates evidence-before-claim. |
| Adopt locally built PrismPM `0.11.1` as canonical verifier | Namespace check passes, but verify fails with `PP5008: required executable lake has an unpinned digest`. | **Rejected.** |
| Add only namespace declarations without upstream stdlib | Would not provide the audited declaration tree required by `prismpm verify`. | **Rejected.** |
| Use the PrismPM `0.5.0` source archive from its checkout instead of the exact binary-embedded archive | Digest `7a1a716a…` does not match the compiled pin; verify fails with `PP5008`. | **Rejected.** |
| Adopt LexLean `0.4.5` | PrismPM `0.5.0` refuses `lexlean/project/2` with `LLC0103`. | **Rejected** (already recorded in ADR-0016). |
| Switch default `rustfmt` without pinning | `rustfmt 1.11.0-stable` breaks SDK pinning under `PP5008`. | **Rejected.** Pin `RUSTUP_TOOLCHAIN=1.97.1` instead. |

## 7. Consequences

### Positive

- The real `prismpm verify` blocker recorded in ADR-0016 D7 is closed with a governed, reproducible pass.
- PrismPM attestation is linked to a LexLean snapshot that contains auditable Prism declarations.
- The canonical toolchain remains unchanged; newer tooling is recorded without silently replacing it.
- Upstream provenance and canonicalization are separated, so future drift is detectable.

### Negative / Trade-offs

- The verified model tree grows from four spike modules to `64` verified modules and requires the upstream stdlib subtree and exporter archive under `RC_ROOT`.
- The adopted `PrismPM` prefix renames the three spike declarations from `RecursiveCognition.*` to `PrismPM.Spike.*`; historical ADR-001/ADR-0016 names are retained only in their own records.
- `prismpm check/verify` now requires a pinned Rust toolchain on this host; the environment is recorded in `tools.lock`.
- PrismPM `0.11.1` and LexLean `0.4.5` remain deferred until SDK pinning and schema compatibility are published.

### Residual risks

| Risk ID | Residual risk | Acceptance gate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `RISK-TOOLCHAIN-DRIFT` | Verification now depends on `RUSTUP_TOOLCHAIN=1.97.1` and a canonical `lexlean` PATH entry. | TS-01…TS-03 once the nine-suite runner exists |
| `RISK-LEXLEAN-IMMATURITY` | LexLean `0.4.3` remains pre-1.0 and bounded to the governed model tree. | Revisit at TRR-2 |
| `RISK-PRISM-MODEL-GAP` | Closed for the governed verify path by this ADR; future model changes require fresh attestations. | Any source/config change re-runs `lexlean verify` and `prismpm verify` |
| `RISK-SDK-PINNING` | Newer PrismPM requires an unpinned executable `lake` on this host. | Re-evaluate `0.11.1` only with pinned SDK evidence |
| `RISK-UNCLAIMED-PHASES` | Kani, nine-suite, dual reports, gates, release, and TRR-2 remain absent. | No claim until each has its own receipts |

## 8. Rollback

1. Restore `lexlean.toml`, `lexlean.lock`, `stdlib/src/`, and `vendor/lean4-prod/lean.tar` from the ADR-0016 governed state; retain `PP5003` receipt as the known blocker.
2. Do not delete this ADR, its evidence register, or its adoption-study receipts; record a superseding rollback reason.
3. Re-run `lexlean verify` and expect the historical ADR-0016 attestation `e2ea04e5e9c899c49fb341ecf555e205208d2e32489e0bf86e2fb57851069bb7`.
4. Re-run `prismpm verify` and expect `PP5003` until equivalent model content is restored.

Rollback is an emergency path only; it reopens the verified-model gap.
